Entry for the make.fun competition "Texts From Your Future Self": one text message under 200 characters from your future self, funny on its own with no setup
`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the gate first, then reading
the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> future you. the gym membership outlived the relationship. cancel one of them.

Why this entry: The joke lands in one breath: a future self reporting a fact only they could know (the gym membership outlived the relationship), then a punchline that turns on ambiguity — cancel one of them, membership or relationship. It is a real text from a future self, specific and lived-in rather than generic, uses every character, and its angle (outliving, not still-broke/still-tired) is fresh.
